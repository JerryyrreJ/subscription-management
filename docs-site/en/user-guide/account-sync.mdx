# Account and sync

SteadyRenew is local-first. You can use it without signing in, or sign in to sync data across devices and unlock cloud-backed features (AI capture, Bark push reminders, Lifetime Premium, and Developer API / MCP keys).

## Without an account

Your data stays in the current browser. This is fast and private, though clearing browser site data or switching devices will not carry your records over unless you export a JSON backup first.

## Sign in & Passkeys

Click **Login to sync** (or the account menu) to sign in with:

- **Email and password**
- **Passkey (WebAuthn)** — sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a hardware security key after enrolling a passkey on your account
- **OAuth providers** (when enabled on the deployment)

Once signed in, SteadyRenew automatically syncs your subscriptions, free trials, custom categories, and Bark notification settings to your account.

## Sync status

The user menu displays your current sync state (syncing, last synced timestamp, sync failed, or never synced). Click **Sync now** anytime to trigger an immediate sync.

## Account settings

Open **Settings → Account** to manage:

- Nickname
- Email address
- Password
- Enrolled **Passkeys** (add or remove passkeys for passwordless sign-in)
- Account deletion (**Danger Zone**)

## Delete account

Open **Settings → Account → Danger Zone** to permanently delete your account. This removes your login credentials, passkeys, personal profile, cloud subscriptions, categories, notification settings, Developer API keys, Lifetime Premium status, and this account's local browser data on the current device. This action cannot be undone.

Deleting an account does not automatically issue a refund. If the account has payment records, SteadyRenew unlinks `user_id` and retains only the payment email and required Stripe transaction fields for financial reconciliation, refunds, payment disputes, and legal record-keeping obligations (never for marketing).

SteadyRenew cannot remotely clear browser storage on other devices you previously used; clear site data manually on those browsers if needed.
