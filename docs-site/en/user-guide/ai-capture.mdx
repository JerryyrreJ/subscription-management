# AI capture

AI capture turns natural-language notes, billing emails, or screenshots into structured subscription actions — adding, updating, deleting, or batch-updating records. You always review the proposed command before anything is saved.

## Requirements & monthly quotas

AI capture requires a **signed-in SteadyRenew account** (so provider keys stay server-side and monthly quotas can be tracked):

| Plan | Monthly AI captures |
| --- | --- |
| Free | **10** successful captures / month |
| Lifetime Premium (\$9 one-time) | **300** successful captures / month |

Only requests that return a valid structured command count toward your monthly quota; failed parses or network errors are not charged.

## Start an AI request

1. Sign in and open **AI Assistant** / **Add with AI**.
2. Type what changed, paste a receipt excerpt, or upload a screenshot (up to 4 MB).
3. Click **Ask AI**.
4. Review the proposed fields (name, amount, currency, period, next renewal or free-trial end date, category).
5. Confirm, edit, discard, or retry.

Examples:

```text
Netflix $15.99 monthly, next charge July 1
Figma Pro 7-day free trial ending 2026-07-10, then $15/month
Change Tencent Cloud to ¥74 yearly
Delete Warmcar
```

## Review and undo

- AI never writes directly to your subscription list; changes are applied only after you click confirm.
- Fields that were inferred or need attention (such as category or custom day intervals) are highlighted for review.
- Right after confirming an AI action, an **Undo** banner appears so you can revert creates, updates, deletes, or batch updates in one click.

## Privacy

The text or screenshot you submit is sent once to the configured model provider solely to extract subscription fields. SteadyRenew does **not** store or log your raw text, screenshots, or parsed items on the server — only per-user monthly capture counts and aggregate token budget counters.
