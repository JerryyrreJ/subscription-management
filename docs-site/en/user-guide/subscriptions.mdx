# Subscriptions

Use subscription records for recurring charges — streaming services, software, cloud hosting, memberships, domains — as well as free trials you want to review before they convert to paid plans.

## Add a subscription

1. Click **Add subscription**.
2. Enter the subscription name and choose (or create) a category.
3. Enter the amount and currency.
4. Choose the billing period: **Monthly**, **Yearly**, or **Custom**.
5. Enter the **next renewal date** (or turn on **Free trial** and pick the **trial end date**).
6. Choose whether Bark push reminders are enabled for this subscription.
7. Save the subscription.

For active recurring subscriptions, the next renewal date automatically advances by full billing cycles once the date passes while preserving your original schedule rule.

## Track a free trial

Turn on the **Free trial** toggle when adding or editing a service that has not billed yet:

- Set the **Trial end / first charge date** and enter the post-trial price (or `0` if you only want a deadline reminder).
- Unlike regular subscriptions, **free trials never roll forward automatically** after the trial end date passes. Expired trials stay highlighted so you can either convert them into an active paid subscription or delete/cancel them after cancelling with the merchant.
- When Bark reminders are enabled, SteadyRenew sends a trial-ending notification ahead of that date.

## Billing periods

| Period | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | Charges that renew on the same calendar day each month |
| Yearly | Charges that renew once a year |
| Custom | Charges that renew every fixed number of days |

### Monthly is a calendar schedule

Monthly billing renews on the same calendar day each month (28–31 days depending on the month). A subscription anchored to the 21st continues on the 21st of every month.

For dates that do not exist in a short month, the app temporarily uses that month's final day and returns to the original anchor afterward:

```text
January 31 → February 28 (or 29) → March 31
```

If a service charges strictly every 30 days instead of on a fixed calendar date, choose **Custom** and enter `30`.

## Edit, pause, or cancel

Open any subscription card to edit its name, category, amount, currency, billing period, next renewal date, trial status, or notification toggle.

You can also mark a subscription as **Paused** or **Cancelled** in the web app if you want to keep its history without counting it toward active monthly/annual spend or sending Bark reminders.

## Filter and sort

Use the dashboard controls to:

- Switch between monthly and yearly totals.
- Change the base display currency.
- Filter by category or status.
- Sort by amount, name, category, next payment date, or creation date (ascending or descending).

## Delete a subscription

Choose **Delete** when you want to permanently remove a record from your tracker. Deleting a record in SteadyRenew (whether in the web app or through the public API / MCP) only removes it from your personal ledger — always cancel the real billing agreement on the merchant's website or App Store / Google Play first.
