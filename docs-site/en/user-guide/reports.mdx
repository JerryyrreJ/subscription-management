# Reports and PDF export

SteadyRenew’s dashboard always shows your monthly and yearly spending overview in your chosen base currency for free. With **Lifetime Premium (\$9 one-time)**, you unlock the **Advanced Report** view and print-ready **A4 PDF exports** (plus duplicate and optimization analytics in the public API / MCP).

## Open the Advanced Report

1. Sign in with an account that has **Lifetime Premium**.
2. On the dashboard, click **View Advanced Report**.

The report converts multi-currency subscriptions into your currently selected base currency for unified totals, trends, and rankings.

## Export a PDF

Two export variants are available at the top of the Advanced Report:

- **Export snapshot** — a compact summary of where your spending stands today: monthly and annualized totals, category breakdown, the subscription table, and charges due in the next 30 days. Long lists automatically flow onto additional A4 pages.
- **Export annual report** — a year-view report with the monthly spend trend, top subscriptions by spend, and category totals across A4 pages.

Choosing an export opens your browser's print dialog with the report laid out on A4 pages. Select **Save as PDF** (or a printer) to finish.

## Notes

- Reports are rendered entirely client-side in your browser; subscription details are never uploaded to a PDF server.
- Exports follow your active app language (English or Simplified Chinese).
- Monthly spend trends are projected from your tracked subscriptions and creation dates rather than bank statement history.
- Every PDF footer stamps the export date and the exchange-rate snapshot used for currency conversion.
