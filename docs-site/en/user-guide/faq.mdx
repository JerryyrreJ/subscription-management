# FAQ

## Where is my data stored?

Without an account, your subscriptions, categories, and preferences stay in your browser's local storage on the current device. When you sign in, SteadyRenew syncs your records to your cloud account so you can access them across devices.

## Do I need an account?

No. Core subscription tracking, multi-currency overview totals, categories, dark mode, and JSON import/export work locally without signing in. Sign in when you want multi-device cloud sync, Passkeys, AI capture, server-sent Bark push reminders, Lifetime Premium, or Developer API / MCP access.

## What is included in Free vs Lifetime Premium?

- **Free (\$0)**: Unlimited tracked subscriptions and free trials, local-first mode + cloud sync, Bark push reminders, **10 AI captures / month**, JSON import/export, and Developer API & MCP access (5 active keys, 60 requests/min, including `get_spend_summary`).
- **Lifetime Premium (\$9 USD one-time — not a subscription)**: Everything in Free, plus **300 AI captures / month**, the **Advanced Report** view with print-ready **A4 PDF exports** (snapshot & annual), and the Premium duplicate/optimization analytics endpoints in the API & MCP.

## Does SteadyRenew connect to my bank or cancel subscriptions for me?

No. SteadyRenew never asks for bank credentials (no Plaid logins) and never logs into external merchants. Deleting or cancelling a record in SteadyRenew only updates your personal tracker — always cancel actual billing directly with the provider, App Store, or Google Play.

## How do free trials behave differently from regular subscriptions?

Regular `active` subscriptions automatically roll their next payment date forward by whole billing periods once a renewal date passes. Free trials (`isTrial: true`) use a one-shot trial end date that **never auto-advances** after expiry, keeping the deadline visible until you convert it to a paid plan or delete it.

## Does AI capture save my screenshot or pasted text?

No. Your text or screenshot is sent once to the configured model provider for extraction and is never stored or logged by SteadyRenew. Only monthly usage counts and aggregate token budget counters are recorded.

## Why do Bark push reminders require signing in?

Bark reminders are sent on a server schedule so they reach your iPhone or iPad even when your browser is closed. Signing in links your synced subscriptions and Bark device URL on the server so scheduled checks know what and where to send.

## Why do totals change when I switch currency, or show an offline rate notice?

The dashboard converts foreign-currency subscriptions into your chosen base currency using live exchange rates (falling back to cached or built-in rates when offline). Your original subscription amounts in their native currencies never change.

## How do I move data to another browser or back up locally?

Open **Settings → General**, click **Export data** to download a JSON backup, and use **Import data** in the target browser.
