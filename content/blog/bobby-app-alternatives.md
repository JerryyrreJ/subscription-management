Bobby won a loyal following for a simple reason: it proved you do not need to hand over your bank password just to remember when Netflix or iCloud renews. Open the app, pick an icon, type the price and billing cycle, and your phone keeps track.

The friction shows up once your list grows past a dozen bills or moves beyond a single iPhone. Reviewing twelve months of credit card PDFs on a laptop while tapping every renewal into a phone screen is slow, Android and Windows devices are left out, and switching ecosystems later gets awkward if you want clean backups or multi-device editing.

## Why people look for a Bobby alternative

Manual subscription tracking works best when the tool matches how you actually review bills and pay for services. Four gaps usually push Bobby users to look elsewhere:

- **iPhone and iPad lock-in:** There is no Android app and no browser workspace, so mixed-device households or anyone auditing bank statements on a Mac or Windows laptop end up retyping numbers from screen to screen.
- **No web-based bulk entry:** When a full [subscription audit checklist](https://steadyrenew.com/blog/how-to-do-a-subscription-audit/) turns up 25 recurring charges across bank PDFs, Apple, and PayPal, entering or editing them on a desktop keyboard takes a fraction of the time.
- **Limited automation or AI drafting:** Phone-only trackers generally require typing every field manually, whereas newer tools let you paste a billing note or screenshot to pre-fill a draft before you review and save it.
- **Cross-currency and reporting needs:** If you earn in one currency and pay for software, servers, or streaming in USD, EUR, GBP, or JPY, checking both the original billed amount and a converted monthly total matters more than a decorative icon grid.

## Four realistic paths after Bobby

### 1. A spreadsheet paired with calendar alerts

If you only check renewals once a month and want complete ownership of your columns, Google Sheets or Excel beats most mobile apps on speed. Set up six columns—service, amount, currency, billing cycle, next renewal date, and payment channel (Visa, Apple, Google Play, PayPal)—then create calendar reminders two to three weeks before large annual bills.

The limitation is upkeep: spreadsheets do not automatically roll monthly or annual renewal dates forward, and you have to remember to add separate calendar events for every free trial.

### 2. Another mobile-only tracker (when you want native iOS/Android widgets)

If you switched from iPhone to Android or prefer native mobile push notifications without opening a browser, Android manual trackers and cross-OS mobile utilities cover basic lists well. Before keying in thirty rows, confirm that free JSON or CSV export is unlocked on day one so you are never locked into a single phone app again.

### 3. Wallos (if you already maintain a home server)

For developers and homelab hobbyists, [Wallos](https://github.com/ellite/Wallos) offers a clean self-hosted web interface with multi-currency conversion, household tags, and webhook/email notifications. The trade-off is running and patching your own container, reverse proxy, and backups just to track recurring bills.

## How to move your list out of Bobby in 15 minutes

1. **Pull your active rows side by side.** Open Bobby on your iPhone alongside your laptop so you can copy each service name, real billed currency, billing cycle, and next renewal date—or take screenshots and use draft capture to speed up entry.
2. **Double-check hidden App Store and PayPal agreements.** While migrating, verify your iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** list and [PayPal automatic payments](https://steadyrenew.com/blog/paypal-automatic-payments-cancel-find/) so nothing billed silently outside Bobby gets missed.
3. **Move yearly renewals and free trials to the top.** Set earlier lead times (2–3 days ahead for free trials, 2–4 weeks ahead for annual plans) so you never catch a charge on the morning it posts.
4. **Save a portable backup.** Export a JSON or CSV copy right after finishing your setup so your list stays yours regardless of which device you switch to next.

## FAQ

### Is there a Bobby app for Android or desktop web?

No. Bobby is an iOS and iPadOS app without an official Android version or web dashboard. If you audit statements on a laptop or use both Android and Apple devices, a browser-based tracker, spreadsheet, or self-hosted web app is much easier to maintain.

### Can a manual tracker automatically import my Bobby subscriptions without a bank login?

Unless your old app exports a clean file format accepted by the new tool, you normally transfer the list once by copying rows or feeding screenshots of your subscription list into an AI draft parser. Taking fifteen minutes during migration is also a good moment to cancel stale plans.

### What is the closest Bobby alternative that does not charge a monthly subscription?

For a mobile-free web workflow with a one-time upgrade option, SteadyRenew covers unlimited subscriptions, cloud sync, Bark reminders, and JSON backups on its Free plan ($0) with an optional $9 USD one-time Premium tier. Plain spreadsheets and self-hosted Wallos also cost $0 in recurring app fees.

### Do any of these alternatives cancel subscriptions automatically?

No. Because privacy-first manual trackers do not hold your bank login or merchant credentials, you always cancel directly in Apple Subscriptions, Google Play, PayPal, or the merchant’s billing page.
